Clean power sources are playing a growing role in how the world generates electricity. Photovoltaic cells inside solar panels turn sunlight straight into usable electric energy. Turbines driven by the wind produce power by harnessing the motion energy of the air. Several governments offer financial support so that families will fit solar panels on their roofs. Over the past ten years, it has become much cheaper to produce electricity from the sun. Still, how much power solar and wind farms deliver is strongly affected by weather conditions. Big batteries are able to keep surplus power and supply it at times of high demand. Authorities are also funding intelligent grids that match supply with demand more effectively. Opponents claim that the upfront price of installing systems is still unaffordable for poor communities. Even so, specialists predict that renewables will lead the growth of new generating capacity in the years ahead.
