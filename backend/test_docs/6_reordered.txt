Despite these challenges, experts expect renewable sources to dominate new power capacity in the coming years. Critics argue that the initial cost of installation remains too high for poorer communities. Governments also invest in smart grids to balance supply and demand more efficiently. Large batteries can store excess electricity and release it when demand is high. However, the output of solar and wind plants depends heavily on the weather. The cost of producing solar electricity has fallen sharply over the last decade. Many countries have introduced subsidies to encourage households to install solar panels. Wind turbines generate electricity by capturing the kinetic energy of moving air. Solar panels convert sunlight directly into electrical power using photovoltaic cells. Renewable energy is becoming an important part of the global electricity supply.
